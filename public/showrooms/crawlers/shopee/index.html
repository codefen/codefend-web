Guest catalog path for Shopee.sg — proven end to end

We started from Shopee.sg’s public sitemaps and extracted product identifiers as shop_id + item_id pairs. Those IDs were stored in SQLite (~9,068 IDs in this PoC—a sample, not the full marketplace catalog) so we could process them in batches without relying on an official API.

Product pages were then opened with Playwright behind a Singapore residential proxy (Proxies.Vision, sticky session). From the guest HTML we read the PDP_BFF_DATA block: title, brand, images, category, stock, description, and color × size variants. That produced a catalog of 62 products and 251 variant rows, at roughly a 69% hit rate. When Shopee issued a traffic verification challenge, we rotated the sticky proxy session and continued.

For pricing, we visited shop listing pages, captured product-card XHR responses (item_card_display_price, micro-units → SGD), and joined them back to the catalog by item_id. ScrapingBee stealth reinforced some shop reads. We obtained real prices on at least five items and closed the PoC loop—without Gmail, Facebook, or phone accounts.

Limitations are inherent to the guest method. Individual product pages do not expose price / units sold reliably in HTML, so those fields are resolved from shop pages (cards / XHR) and joined by item_id. It works, but it means crawling shops in addition to PDPs. This PoC did not aim for full price / sold coverage across all 62 items—the bar was to prove the loop. our_price landed at 5/62, and list_price / units_sold at 4/62 via the same path. Production coverage means crawling more shops. Occasional traffic challenges (verify) are mitigated by rotating the sticky proxy session.

The brief asks for 18 fields per product / variant. Across 62 products and 251 variants, core catalog fields closed near 100%. Gaps in price / sold / list are coverage gaps (crawl more shops with the same method), not fields impossible in guest mode. Scaling = more shop crawl, same join by item_id.

Key findings

  • No accounts required — full path without Gmail, Facebook, phone, or Shopee login
  • 62 products · 251 variants · ~69% PDP hit rate
  • Useful spend ≈ USD 0.50–2 to close catalog + prices
